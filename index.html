The garments

Placement

Put it exactly where it goes.

Drag, pinch and twist right on the garment. What you see is where it prints.

  • Snaps to centre so a chest print sits straight, every time.
  • Live size in inches, so you know the real print size.
  • Up to six layers per side: a chest logo, a back graphic and a sleeve mark on one garment.
  • Sliders for size and rotation, and a flat 2D view for precise work.
Colour

Every part, any colour.

Colour the body, sleeves, hood and pocket separately. Pick from the shades blanks really come in, or match any colour with the eyedropper.

  • Ten fabric shades from white and bone to forest, navy and maroon.
  • Recolour the artwork too, outline and fill, to match your brand.
  • Studio and photo backdrops, or any plain colour behind the garment.
